# Disposition and handling procedure

## When an item is judged spoiled
1. Do not taste it. Tasting to confirm spoilage is never an accepted control.
2. Isolate it from other stock immediately; mould sporulates and cross-contaminates.
3. Bag it before binning to prevent spore and liquid spread.
4. Clean and sanitise every surface, container and utensil it touched.
5. Wash hands after handling.
6. Inspect adjacent items in the same container or shelf — spoilage propagates
   by contact and by shared humidity.

## When an item is judged fresh
A fresh verdict clears *appearance only*. Before use, confirm the item is within
its use-by date, has held temperature control, and shows no off odour. A use-by
date is a safety limit and is not overridden by a clean visual inspection; a
best-before date is a quality limit and can be.

## When the assessment is uncertain
Route to a human inspector. Do not release the item on an uncertain automated
verdict, and do not discard it automatically either — abstention exists so that
a person applies judgement, not so the decision is deferred indefinitely.
