# Time and temperature control

Most bacterial growth in food happens in the danger zone between 5 °C and 60 °C
(41 °F–140 °F).

* Perishables should spend no more than **2 hours** cumulatively in the danger
  zone; **1 hour** when ambient temperature is above 32 °C (90 °F).
* Refrigerate at or below 5 °C; freeze at or below −18 °C.
* Cooked leftovers should be cooled to refrigeration temperature within 2 hours
  and consumed within 3–4 days.
* Freezing halts growth but does not kill most pathogens; the count resumes on
  thaw. Thaw in the fridge, not on the counter.

An item that has breached these controls is discarded regardless of appearance,
which is why a vision model's output is only ever one input to a disposition
decision.
