# The limits of visual inspection

The most dangerous foodborne pathogens produce no visible change at the
concentrations that cause illness.

* *Salmonella*, *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Campylobacter* and pathogenic
  *E. coli* do not reliably alter colour, texture or odour.
* *Listeria* grows at refrigeration temperature, so "it has been cold the whole
  time" is not a defence.
* Some *Bacillus cereus* and *Staphylococcus aureus* toxins survive cooking, so
  reheating a mishandled item does not make it safe.

The practical consequence: a "fresh" verdict from any image-based system is a
statement about *visible* spoilage only. It cannot clear an item that has been
time-temperature abused, recalled, or handled in an unsanitary way. Those are
process controls, not perception problems.
